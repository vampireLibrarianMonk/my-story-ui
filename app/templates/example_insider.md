# Example character - The Insider

> Reference example, not canon. One of a contrasting pair (see "Example character - The Outsider") that
> shows how two characters can relate at several levels at once. Copy the structure, replace the content.

## 1. Identity
- **Name:** Captain Vehl Mara (example)
- **Age / stage:** mid-career officer
- **Role in the story:** institutional insider; the face of an established order
- **In one line:** a dutiful officer who mistakes the institution's survival for justice.

## 2. Physical presence
- **Appearance:** uniform kept immaculate; everything else, OPEN.
- **Bearing:** controlled, formal, watchful in a room.

## 3. Background and history
- **Origin:** raised inside the institution she now serves.
- **Formative events:** promoted for loyalty during a crisis she helped cover.
- **What they carry:** the belief that order is the highest mercy.

## 4. Inner life and personality
- **Temperament:** measured, procedural.
- **Values:** duty, hierarchy, continuity.
- **Contradiction:** privately doubts the order she publicly defends.

## 5. Want versus need
- **Want:** to rise within the institution and reform it from inside.
- **Need:** to learn that an unjust order is not worth preserving.

## 6. Wound, lie, and fear
- **Wound:** punished once for questioning a superior.
- **Lie believed:** "working within the system is the only safe way to change it."
- **Fear:** being cast out and powerless.

## 7. Relationships
- See the relationship records linking her to The Outsider. The point of this pair is that they relate at
  several levels at once, and those levels disagree.

## 8. Voice and mannerisms
- Speaks in regulations and precedent; softens only in private.

## 9. Capabilities and limits
- **Strengths:** authority, access, institutional knowledge.
- **Limits:** her power evaporates the moment she defies the institution.

## 10. Arc and transformation
- From enforcer of the order toward a reformer who risks her standing.

## 11. Role in theme
- Tests the idea that loyalty and justice can diverge.

## 12. Open questions and status
- **Decided:** role as insider; the insider/outsider tension.
- **Open:** name, appearance, the institution's nature, the outcome of her arc.
- **Proposed:** everything specific above is a draft to replace.
