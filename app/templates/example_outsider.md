# Example character - The Outsider

> Reference example, not canon. The contrasting half of a pair (see "Example character - The Insider").
> Where the Insider is defined by rank and belonging, the Outsider is defined by personal loyalty and
> conviction. Their ties span every relationship scale, and the scales do not agree with each other.

## 1. Identity
- **Name:** Rence of the Low Road (example)
- **Age / stage:** indeterminate; has lived outside institutions for years
- **Role in the story:** independent outsider; conscience without a badge
- **In one line:** a free agent whose loyalty is to people, not to orders.

## 2. Physical presence
- **Appearance:** travel-worn, deliberately unmarked by any insignia; details OPEN.
- **Bearing:** easy, disarming, hard to pin down.

## 3. Background and history
- **Origin:** left or was cast out of the same institution the Insider serves.
- **Formative events:** survived by building a web of personal debts and favors.
- **What they carry:** a grudge against the order, and a stubborn hope for the people in it.

## 4. Inner life and personality
- **Temperament:** improvisational, warm, wary of authority.
- **Values:** loyalty to persons, freedom, keeping one's word.
- **Contradiction:** claims to trust no institution, yet keeps trying to save the people inside one.

## 5. Want versus need
- **Want:** to stay free and owe nothing to any order.
- **Need:** to accept that some structures are worth mending, not just escaping.

## 6. Wound, lie, and fear
- **Wound:** betrayed by the institution that should have protected them.
- **Lie believed:** "belonging to anything is a trap."
- **Fear:** being bound into service again.

## 7. Relationships
- See the relationship records linking them to The Insider: a personal bond that cuts across an
  organizational and societal divide, with opposed ideological allegiances.

## 8. Voice and mannerisms
- Speaks plainly, deflects with humor, remembers every debt owed and owing.

## 9. Capabilities and limits
- **Strengths:** networks, improvisation, moving unseen between factions.
- **Limits:** no institutional power; relies entirely on personal trust, which can break.

## 10. Arc and transformation
- From lone operator toward someone who chooses to belong on their own terms.

## 11. Role in theme
- Tests whether freedom without belonging is a virtue or a wound.

## 12. Open questions and status
- **Decided:** role as outsider; the personal-vs-organizational tension with the Insider.
- **Open:** name, appearance, the exact history with the institution, the outcome.
- **Proposed:** everything specific above is a draft to replace.
